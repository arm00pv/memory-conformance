# Does every question measure something?

Generated by `items.py`. Do not edit by hand. Analysed in the **written** phrasing.

A question every subject answers and a question no subject answers carry the same information
about the differences between subjects: none. This is the item analysis a test gets before
anyone trusts it - difficulty `p` (the share of subjects that got it right) and discrimination
`D` (the gap between the top half of subjects by total score and the bottom half).

## Reading this

A low `p` is not a defect: a question the weaker subjects miss is doing its job. A `p` of 0 or 1
is, because nothing about the subjects can be learned from it - including the leader, whose
score those items inflate for everyone equally.

`D` is crude on purpose. With ten subjects a point-biserial correlation wears a false air of
precision; "the strong subjects get this and the weak ones do not" is what a question is for.
