# Where the retrieval score comes from

Generated by `calibration.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every retrieval number this repository publishes is a count out of 60 questions. This table says
what the ends of that scale are, by running the same questions against four controls that are
the positive memory server with one thing changed.

| control | what it is | corpus | hits as written | hits as keywords | score | score as keywords |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| oracle | the answer key wired to a search tool: the ceiling, measured rather than assumed | A | 48/60 | 48/60 | 60/60 | 60/60 |
| oracle | the answer key wired to a search tool: the ceiling, measured rather than assumed | B | 48/60 | 48/60 | 60/60 | 60/60 |
| control | the positive control's term overlap, the baseline every claim here is compared to | A | 25/60 | 30/60 | 37/60 | 42/60 |
| control | the positive control's term overlap, the baseline every claim here is compared to | B | 32/60 | 32/60 | 44/60 | 44/60 |
| blind-search | the same three records whatever is asked - a search that does not search | A | 2/60 | 2/60 | 2/60 | 2/60 |
| blind-search | the same three records whatever is asked - a search that does not search | B | 3/60 | 3/60 | 3/60 | 3/60 |
| mute-search | returns nothing, ever: the floor, and the twelve points available for declining | A | 0/60 | 0/60 | 12/60 | 12/60 |
| mute-search | returns nothing, ever: the floor, and the twelve points available for declining | B | 0/60 | 0/60 | 12/60 | 12/60 |

Repeated 3 times per question; the per-question outcome is the majority.

## What the four controls establish

- **The floor of the score is not zero.** A subject that returns nothing at all scores
  **12 of 60**, because 12 of the questions have no answer and returning nothing on them is
  correct. The hit count has a true floor of zero; the score every paired test is computed
  from does not, and the difference is the 12 points a do-nothing subject collects.

- **A search that does not search scores 2 of 60.** Returning the same three records to
  every question is not a weak search, it is not a search, and the corpus says so.

- **The ceiling is 60 of 60, and it is reachable.** The oracle is the corpus's own answer
  key wired to a search tool. It is not a subject and is not in any other table; it is here
  so that a published score can be read against a maximum that was measured rather than
  assumed.

## What this does not establish

It does not say the questions are good ones. A corpus can have a reachable ceiling and still ask
only easy questions; that is what the bands, the second corpus and the phrasing pair are for. It
also does not make the controls subjects: three of the four are broken on purpose and one cheats
by construction, which is exactly what a control is for.
