Generated by `agent.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Four questions can be asked of the same saved rows, and this repository has been publishing one
of them:

| question | the right record in the top three | the right record first | credits a correct silence |

| `top` | no | yes | yes |

Every paired test in this repository is computed from `score`. `top` is
what a caller with a token budget sees, and it is the only one of the four that is also what a
caller experiences: you ask a question, you read the first line back, and when the memory has
nothing you get nothing.

## What this does not say

It does not say the score is wrong to credit the top three. A memory asked to surface candidates
is doing its job when the right candidate is among them, and a caller that reads all three gets
what it paid for. It says that the table has one number in it and four questions behind it, and
that the one published is the only one of the four that no caller experiences directly.
